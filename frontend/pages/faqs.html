Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I submit my art?
First navigate to the Home page and click on the hyperlinked text that reads: Artist Submission Form. After clicking the link there will be a form that will can be submitted to be reviewed and added to the site.

Who can submit art on this site?
Any artist who wishes to use the platform to share their artwork.

Do I need an account?
There are no accounts that need to be made to view the website. Submitting art will require an email.

Is it Free? ArtConnect is completely free to use.

What is ArtConnect and how do you use it?
ArtConnect is a site to both view and share art and art related events. To view artwork click on the Gallery page and to view art events click on the Events page.
